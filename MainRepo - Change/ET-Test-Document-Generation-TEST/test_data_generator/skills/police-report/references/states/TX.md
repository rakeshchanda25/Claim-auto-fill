## Non-crash incidents

The CR-3 is a *crash* form. For property scenarios (fire, water, theft, wind) the layout keeps the
TxDOT masthead, the 3.1/3.2 identification bands and 4.4/4.7/4.8, and replaces 3.3 with a
reporting-party and incident-detail block. Sections 4.2 and 4.3 are omitted — there is no injured
party disposition and no charge.
